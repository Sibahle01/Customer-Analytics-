Welcome to the my Customer Segmentation Analysis Project. This comprehensive platform provides deep insights into customer behavior patterns, segmentation, and fraud detection to drive strategic decision-making.

Methodology

This segmentation approach combines k-means clustering with principal component analysis to identify distinct customer groups based on spending patterns, transaction frequency, and demographic attributes.

The fraud detection system utilizes isolation forests and gradient boosting to identify anomalous transactions with high precision while minimizing false positives.

Key Findings & Strategic Insights

Confidential

Segment Distribution

The customer base is evenly distributed across all four segments, with Budget Savers slightly leading at 25.5%, followed by High-Value Loyalists (25.4%), Young Professionals (25.3%), and Occasional Users (23.8%).

Recommendation: Develop balanced marketing strategies that address the unique needs of each segment while maintaining broad appeal.

Age Demographics

High-Value Loyalists tend to be older (60-70 years) than other segments, while Young Professionals have the lowest median age (around 38). Budget Savers and Occasional Users have similar age distributions (median age 42-47).

Recommendation: Tailor communication channels and messaging by age group - digital-first for Young Professionals, more traditional for High-Value Loyalists.

Spending Patterns

Budget Savers have the highest total spending (approximately ZAR 26,000) despite their value consciousness, followed by High-Value Loyalists (ZAR 20,000). Young Professionals spend the least (ZAR 16,000).

Recommendation: Develop loyalty programs for Budget Savers to maintain their engagement, and targeted upsell strategies for Young Professionals to increase their spending.

Fraud Timing Patterns

Fraud rates peak at 11:00 (0.58%) and 13:00 (0.56%), with a significant dip around 18:00 (0.32%). This suggests fraudsters may be targeting times when transaction volumes are high to blend in.

Recommendation: Implement dynamic fraud detection thresholds that adjust based on time of day, with stricter rules during peak fraud hours.

Regional Fraud Patterns

Eastern Cape shows the highest fraud rate at 0.50%, followed by Limpopo (0.48%) and Gauteng (0.47%). Free State has the lowest fraud rate at 0.27%.

Recommendation: Develop region-specific fraud prevention strategies, with additional verification steps for high-risk provinces and optimized processes for low-risk areas.

Fraud Detection Features

Our machine learning model identified V14 as the most important feature for fraud detection (importance: 0.27), followed by V7 (0.20). These anonymized transaction features provide strong signals for potential fraud.

Recommendation: Focus real-time monitoring on transactions with abnormal V14 and V7 values, and investigate these features further to understand their business meaning.
